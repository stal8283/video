# GTA 6 Shorts: Reference Breakdown + Content Plan

## 1. Reference video breakdown

**Video:** @1deflay, "Get $50,000 AND a LEGENDARY AR in GTA 6! (Secret Location)", posted 2026-11-22
**Stats after about 2 days:** 2.8M views · 383.6K likes (**13.7%** like rate) · 53.4K shares (**1.9%**) · 1.6K comments
**Length:** 108 s, 1080x1920, 60 fps, voiceover with game audio underneath

The like and share rates are very high. People saved and sent it because it is **useful**: a guide they plan to follow. Every idea below copies that.

### Structure (timestamps from the actual video)

| Time | Beat | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–4.4 | **Hook** | "Here's how you can get **$50,000** in loot and a **legendary AR** in GTA VI in **2 minutes**." Two rewards, one time frame. The picture is only the character walking; the words carry the hook. |
| 4.7–13.5 | **Location** | Map shown for about 1.5 s ("right above Little Cuba in Vice City"), then the arrival. Viewers pause and screenshot this. |

### Formula
1. **Specific numbers everywhere.** Dollar amounts are said out loud about 15 times. Every step pays off with a figure.
2. **A promise in the first sentence, proven at the end.** The final line repeats the hook's number.
3. **A re-hook every 20–25 s** ("not even the craziest", "we're not done yet").
4. **A step-by-step route** with easy instructions ("no spikes on this wall", "if you have a suppressed weapon no one gets alerted"). Viewers feel they can do it themselves.
5. **Proof on screen.** Red arrows on HUD cash pop-ups, and the item values are visible in game.
6. **A plain edit.** Only about 12 hard cuts in 108 s. Most of it is continuous gameplay with jump cuts that remove walking. The voice sets the pace.

### Visual style
- 16:9 gameplay **cropped to about 1080x1220** in the middle of the 9:16 frame, with a **blurred copy of the same footage** filling the top and bottom. No black bars.
- **One word on screen at a time**: white, heavy condensed bold font, thick black outline, centered near the top of the gameplay window. Numbers appear as typed figures ("$12,600").
- **Red arrows** on HUD reward pop-ups, held for about 1–2 s.
- Voiceover: fast (about 3 words/s), confident, plain. No visible facecam, no on-screen title card.

### What we can do better than the reference
- **Colour-code the payoff words.** Money in green, hype words in yellow. The reference keeps everything white. `shortmaker` does this automatically.
- **Show the map longer** (about 2.5 s) with a pin or arrow on it. It is the most-paused frame, so give it room.
- **Keep a running total on screen** during the loot section.
- **Comment bait at the end**, e.g. "Comment the district you want next". The reference only has 1.6K comments against 383K likes, so comments are the weak spot.

## 2. Content ideas (ranked by how closely they follow the proven formula)

### Tier 1: same formula, new locations (make these first)
1. **"$X Loot House" series, one per district.** Little Cuba was done, so cover Vice Beach, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, Grassrivers, Leonida Keys and Mount Kalaga. Same structure every time. Title template: *"Get $X AND a [rare item] in GTA 6 (Secret Location)"*.
2. **"Cars with NO tracker that sell for the most."** The reference mentioned this only in passing ("unlocked with no tracker, sells for $8,500"). Make it the main subject: top 5 sellable spawns, each with its location and the sell-value screen as proof.
3. **"Every LEGENDARY weapon you can get early."** One weapon per short (a series) or a top-5 compilation. The hook is the weapon's name and stats, plus "before mission X".

### Tier 2: same money hook, different structure
4. **"Money per minute" tests.** "I tested 5 ways to make money in GTA 6. Here's which pays the most per minute." A stopwatch and dollar total on screen per method, ending on a ranking. Easy to turn into a recurring series.
5. **"You're losing money if you don't do this."** Mechanics explainers: the stealth bonus (seen in the reference: +$2,761 for a silent takedown), fencing jewelry and watches, sell timing. Short (30–45 s), with a high share rate.
6. **"Do this BEFORE you finish the story."** Missable loot, cars and weapons. The fear of missing out drives saves.

### Tier 3: growth and variety
7. **"Secret spots nobody found yet."** Easter eggs and hidden rooms, using the same location-reveal structure but with curiosity as the reward instead of money.
8. **"Rarest outfits and where to get them."** Lucia/Jason fashion items. Reaches a different audience segment.
9. **Myth-test shorts.** "Can you survive X?" or "What happens if you Y?" Short, comment-heavy, and a good balance to the guide content.

### Posting rhythm
- 1 Tier-1 short per day (the series keeps people coming back), plus a Tier-2 or Tier-3 short every other day.
- Use the same title template, caption style and voice every time, so viewers recognise the channel.
- Pin a comment with the map screenshot on every guide.

## 3. Ready-to-record scripts (about 3 words/s ≈ 100–110 s)

Each script follows the reference beat-by-beat. Fill in `[brackets]` with what you find while recording, and capture the listed shots.

### Script A: Loot house (template)
> Here's how you can get **$[TOTAL]** AND a **legendary [ITEM]** in GTA 6 in **two minutes**.
> Make your way to this exact spot, right [next to / above] **[LANDMARK]** in **[DISTRICT]**. *(MAP shot, 2.5 s)*
> When you get here, [entry detail]. There are no [guards / cameras] on this side, so it's a free entry.
> First, [step 1 + reward, e.g. "this unlocked [CAR] with no tracker that sells for $[X]"], but that's not even the best part.
> [Step 2 → reward]. [Step 3 → reward, stealth tip]. *(ARROW on the HUD pop-up)*
> But we're not done yet. [Step 4: the guarded room]. Take them out with a suppressed weapon and nobody gets alerted.
> In the back is the **legendary [ITEM]**. And on this table, two stash bags, worth **$[X]** each.
> That puts us at **$[RUNNING TOTAL]** already. Grab [last items], then leave through [exit] and take the [CAR].
> So that's **$[A]** in stash, **$[B]** in cash and **$[C]** for the car. Basically **$[TOTAL]** from one house. Comment which district you want next.

**Shots to capture:** walking intro (5 s), map with waypoint, entry point, each loot pickup with the HUD pop-up visible, the guarded room, the legendary weapon, the stash bags, the exit, driving away. Record with the HUD on.

### Script B: Cars with no tracker

### Script C: Money-per-minute test
> I tested **5 ways to make money** in GTA 6 and timed every one. The winner made **$[X] per minute**.
> *(For each method: name → stopwatch on screen → "I made $[X] in [Y] minutes, that's $[Z] per minute")*
> Re-hook at #3: "This next one completely changed my ranking."
> Final ranking on screen. "Comment if you found something faster."
