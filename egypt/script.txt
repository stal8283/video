Ten weird things you didn't know about ancient Egyptians.
Number ten. They invented toothpaste, made from crushed ox hooves, burnt eggshells and ash.
Number nine. Ancient writers said that when a pet cat died, the whole family shaved off their eyebrows to mourn it.
Number eight. To test if a woman was pregnant, she peed on bags of wheat and barley. If they sprouted, she was pregnant. And scientists later found it actually kind of worked.
Number seven. The pyramids weren't built by slaves. They were built by paid workers, and part of their pay was beer. Lots of beer.
But it gets even weirder.
Number six. Tomb builders went on the first recorded strike in history, because their food rations were late.
Number five. Their bread was so full of sand and grit, it ground their teeth down.
Number four. Men and women both wore heavy eye makeup, and it may have actually protected their eyes from infections.
Number three. For birth control, some women used crocodile dung.
Number two. To make a mummy, they pulled the brain out through the nose with a hook, and threw it away.
But the weirdest part of all?
Number one. Legend says one pharaoh covered his servants in honey, so the flies would bite them instead of him.
