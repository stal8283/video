- **Model:** Lucid Realism (or Phoenix). **Style:** Cinematic / None. **Ratio:** 9:16. **Images per prompt:** 1.
- **Turn off:** Ultra, Alchemy and upscalers.
- **Name each download with its code** (`E1a.png`, `B5.png`…) so I know which is which.
- **Characters:** whole body in frame, plain bright green background, no green clothing. Redo any image with a green tint on the person or a floor/shadow behind them.
- **"a" / "b" codes:** the same person in two poses. Keep the prompt text identical apart from the pose so it stays the same person.

Add this to the end of **every character prompt**:
> `, entire body visible from head to feet, isolated on a plain solid bright green background, even studio lighting, photorealistic, sharp focus`
