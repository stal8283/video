Imagine you're one of three men guarding a lighthouse on a remote island, and all three of you vanish without a trace. Well, this actually happened in 1900, on the Flannan Isles, off the coast of Scotland. It started when a passing ship noticed something strange. The lighthouse was completely dark. But brutal storms meant no one could reach the island for eleven days. And when the relief boat finally arrived, nobody came down to meet them. No flag. No signal. Nothing. So the relief keeper climbed up alone, and found the gate and the door shut tight. Inside, the beds were empty, the clock had stopped, and the fireplace was stone cold. But here's where it gets weird. Two of the men's waterproof coats were gone. But the third was still hanging on its hook. Meaning one man ran outside, into a winter storm, in just his shirt. Up on the cliffs, iron railings were bent, and a box of ropes kept over a hundred feet above the sea had vanished. Investigators blamed a giant wave. But not a single body was ever found.
