# Leonardo prompts: Flannan Isles short

## Settings (to make the free tokens go further)

- **Model:** Leonardo **Lucid Realism**, or **Phoenix 1.0** if Lucid isn't available. Style: **Cinematic** or **None**.
- **Aspect ratio:** **9:16** (portrait) for everything.
- **Number of images:** **1** per prompt. Regenerate only if it comes out wrong.
- **Turn off** Ultra, Alchemy and upscalers. They cost extra tokens and I can sharpen the images here.
- Paste each prompt exactly as written.
- **Name each download** with the code in brackets, e.g. `C1.png`, so I know which is which.

## Rules for the CHARACTERS (green-background cutouts)

I'll cut each person out of the green background, put them into the scenes, and draw the cartoon head over their real head. For that to work:
- The **whole body** must be in frame, from the top of the hat to the boots, with a little space around it. No cropped feet.
- The background must be **plain bright green with nothing else in it**.
- **No green clothing.**
- If an image comes out with a green tint on the person, or a floor or shadow, regenerate it.
